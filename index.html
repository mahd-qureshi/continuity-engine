Behind the story

What makes it continuity?

Mara’s next scene depends on what you’ve already chosen. Starting an email together can open a later option. Avoiding a conversation can change how it begins.

Every decision is recorded. Rewind to one of them and the story rebuilds from that point. Finish two paths to see what changed.

Small by design

One authored story. Five decisions. No generated dialogue, model calls, or account required. Your progress stays in this browser; sharing a link includes your choices.
